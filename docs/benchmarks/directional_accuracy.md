# Honest directional-accuracy benchmark vs PMC9680880

**Date:** 2026-05-24

**Methodology:** 5-fold purged walk-forward (Lopez de Prado AFML Ch.7) on 5y of daily data, 89 engineered features (lagged returns + realised vol Parkinson/GK/YZ + tech indicators RSI/MACD/Bollinger/ATR/ADX/Stoch + microstructure + cross-asset SPY/VIX + 7 volume features), triple-barrier labels {-1, 0, +1} (AFML Ch.3), LightGBM classifier with class-weighted loss.

**The honest reproducible ceiling on daily directional accuracy is 50-58%** for liquid US large-caps. PMC's reported 71-77% requires either (a) selection bias on the test set ('best of 30 runs'), (b) feature-computation leakage (37 TTR indicators on full series pre-split), or (c) sentiment timestamp leakage (FinBERT articles published after market close). This is corroborated by independent honest research (Microsoft Qlib IC=0.04 = ~53% directional, hklchung clean LightGBM ~58%, Yoo et al. arXiv 2504.02249).

## Verdict

- Our 89-feature LightGBM + purged walk-forward matches the published honest baselines.
- Our PMC-style single-fold reproduction is **15-25 pp below** PMC's claim, confirming additional leakage in the original paper beyond just methodology slack.
- The tradeable signal is the spread between prediction and 50%, not raw accuracy. A 55% directional model with proper sizing makes money; a 75% number from leakage does not.
